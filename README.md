# Heimdall

**Instant, private voice dictation for Windows.** Press a hotkey, speak, and your words are typed wherever your cursor is: emails, documents, chats, code editors, anywhere.

Heimdall runs entirely on your own computer. Your voice and your text never leave it.

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/TheDarkCitadel/Heimdall-releases/releases/latest)**

Under **Assets**, click `HeimdallSetup-x.y.z.exe`, then run it.

> Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen because Heimdall is new. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What you need

- Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11
- An NVIDIA graphics card with up-to-date drivers
- About 3 GB of free disk space
- A microphone

## Getting started

1. Run the installer and launch Heimdall.
2. The first time, Heimdall downloads its GPU speech libraries (about 1.8 GB). This happens only once.
3. Follow the short setup: pick your microphone and your hotkey.
4. Click into any text box, press your hotkey, and start talking. Press it again to stop, or just stop talking and Heimdall stops on its own.

Heimdall lives in the system tray, near the clock. Click its icon for quick options, Settings, or to exit.

## Features

- **Dictation anywhere:** one hotkey, typed straight into whatever app you're using.
- **Fully private:** speech recognition runs locally on your GPU. No account and no cloud.
- **Around 99 languages:** speak in your language, or have it translated to English as you talk.
- **System audio:** transcribe what your computer is playing, like calls and videos.
- **Auto-stop:** stops listening after a pause, with a delay you choose, or never.
- **The eye:** a small floating overlay that shows when Heimdall is listening. Pick its style, color and size.
- **Automatic updates:** Heimdall tells you when a new version is ready and installs it for you.
